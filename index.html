Kalshi research bots

Each bot focuses on one kind of Kalshi market, publishes its picks, and keeps a public record of every result, wins and losses alike. A bot is trusted in a city only after its record shows an edge over the market’s own prices.

How they’re judged

Every bot is backtested month by month using only data that existed at the time, with Kalshi’s fees included. Its live picks are logged at the prices it saw and scored when markets settle. A city or time of day earns “proven edge” only after at least 30 bets, with forecasts more accurate than the market and a 90% range on profit that stays above zero.
